Terms of Service

Last updated: 30 October 2026

The service

DDPSourcing sources products from suppliers in China and delivers them to clients in Morocco on a DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) basis: supplier sourcing, quality inspection, express air freight, customs clearance, import duties, and door-to-door delivery are included in one final price. Invoices are issued by Beast Capital Group.

Requests and prices

  • Sending a request is free and does not oblige you to order.
  • After review, we send a final price per unit and a minimum quantity. The price is based on the product, quantity, specifications, packaging, and delivery address you approve, and is valid for 7 days.
  • If you change any of these before confirming, we send an updated price.
  • We may decline any request. Illegal, counterfeit, prohibited, or unsafe products, and products not allowed on express air freight, are not accepted.

Payment

  • Your order is confirmed when you pay a deposit of 40% of the total amount. We then order your stock from the supplier.
  • The remaining 60% is due once your goods have cleared customs in Morocco, before delivery. Goods are delivered after the balance is received.
  • Payments are made by bank transfer only, to the account of Beast Capital Group.

Delivery time

We ship by express air only. Delivery normally takes 15 to 20 days from the confirmation of your order (40% deposit). Delays caused by events outside our control, such as supplier production issues, customs inspections, airline disruptions, or public holidays, may extend this time. We inform you as soon as we know of any delay.

Quality and claims

Goods are inspected before shipping against the specifications agreed in your order. If you receive goods that do not match the agreed specifications, contact us as soon as possible after delivery with photos or videos, and we will find a solution with you.

Cancellation

You can cancel free of charge before paying the 40% deposit. Once we have ordered your stock from the supplier, the order can no longer be cancelled.

Your responsibilities

You confirm that the products you request can be legally sold in Morocco and do not infringe anyone's trademark or intellectual property, and that the information you give us (address, contact, specifications) is correct.

Governing law

These terms are governed by Moroccan law. Any dispute falls under the competent Moroccan courts.
