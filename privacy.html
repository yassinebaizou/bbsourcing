Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Who we are

DDPSourcing provides product sourcing and express import services from China to Morocco. DDPSourcing is a service of Beast Capital Group, Casablanca, which issues the invoices. Contact: contact@ddpsourcing.com.

What we collect

When you create an account or send a request through our website, we collect:

  • Your email address and account details (your password is stored encrypted; we cannot read it).
  • Your full name, WhatsApp number, city, and, if you give them, your store name or link.
  • Product information: product link, product name, quantity, photos, and any details you add.
  • Your consent to be contacted on WhatsApp and to this policy, and the date and time of your request.

If you become a client, we also keep order, delivery, and payment records (amounts, dates, payment references) needed for the service and for accounting.

Why we use it

  • To review your request, find a supplier, and send you a price.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp about your request and order.
  • To manage your order, customs clearance, delivery, invoices, and payments.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for unrelated advertising.

Who can see it

Only our team members who handle your request. When needed to deliver the service, we share the minimum necessary information with suppliers, shipping and customs partners, and delivery companies. Your account, requests, pictures and conversations are stored with Supabase, on servers in the European Union (Ireland). Emails about your account are sent through Resend, and the website is hosted by Vercel.

How long we keep it

Order and payment records are kept for as long as Moroccan accounting and tax rules require.

Your rights

Under Moroccan Law 09-08 on the protection of personal data, you can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and you can object to its use for legitimate reasons. Send your request to contact@ddpsourcing.com. You can also contact the CNDP (Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel).

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
